# Mainline Next Guidelines

All implementation, API, architecture, testing, and documentation decisions in
`mainline-next/` must conform to
[`docs/design-principles.md`](docs/design-principles.md).

Before making a decision, review the applicable design principles and preserve
their intent, not only their literal wording. If a proposed change conflicts
with a principle or exposes an ambiguity between principles, do not silently
make an exception. Document the conflict and resolve it in the design documents
before proceeding.
